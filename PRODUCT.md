# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui — confirmed by the user.

## Users

Procurement staff (снабженцы) and technologists at production companies in Uzbekistan — confectionery, meat processing, textile and paper plants. They come to compare price per kg, minimum batch and availability for a specific raw material, then call or request a callback to place an order.

## Product Purpose

RTW (Right To Win) is a wholesale supplier of starch, molasses and syrups, and technical chemistry. The site's catalog lets a buyer find the right raw material quickly, see the wholesale price and minimum order, and reach a manager. Success: a qualified buyer calls or leaves a callback request.

## Positioning

Stock is physically held at RTW's own warehouse in Tashkent: goods are in stock and ship right away, with free delivery within the city. This is the claim to lead with (confirmed by the user).

## Operating Context

Buyers usually know the material they need (e.g. "крахмал кукурузный") and check price, batch size and availability against other suppliers, often on a phone between other tasks. Orders are closed by phone with a manager, not through online checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Catalog with categories: Крахмал, Патока и сиропы, Химия; category filter; product cards with category, name, stock status, price in UZS (сум) per kg, minimum order.
- Contact: phone +998 99 392 23 05, email info@rtw.uz, "Оставить заявку" / "Перезвоните мне" callback request.
- No online checkout or cart payment.
- Language: Russian.
- Undecided: product photography (none provided for the rebuild), real callback backend (form posts nowhere yet).

## Brand Commitments

Name "RTW / Right To Win", tagline "крахмал и патока оптом". The existing site uses a red accent; the user has not made a specific palette binding.

## Evidence on Hand

Real catalog data from the current site (7 products, prices, minimum orders, stock status) and contacts — user confirmed they own the site and the content may be reused. No testimonials, client logos, certificates or delivery statistics were provided: do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Price, minimum batch and availability are the decision — they must be readable at a glance.
2. Lead with the Tashkent warehouse: in stock, ships now, free city delivery.
3. Every screen is one step from a phone call or a callback request.
4. Truthful and specific: no invented claims, numbers or social proof.
